Important

CycleTrack provides estimates and pattern tracking, not a diagnosis or guarantee of ovulation, fertility, pregnancy, or infertility. A positive ovulation test usually indicates an LH surge, but it does not prove that ovulation occurred. A sustained basal-temperature rise can provide retrospective evidence of an ovulation pattern. Fertile-type cervical mucus is a useful fertility sign, but timing varies between people and cycles. Do not use this calendar alone as contraception.
